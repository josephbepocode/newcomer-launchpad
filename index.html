Getting started online

Learn the basics, set up email and stay safe from scams.

Computer & email basics

  • GCFGlobal free tutorialsComputers, email, internet basics — free, self-paced

Tip: create one professional email (e.g. firstname.lastname) for job searching and official forms.

Online safety

  • Get Cyber Safe (Government of Canada)Passwords, phishing, securing your devices

Tip: use a different strong password for each account and turn on two-step verification.

Spotting scams

  • Canadian Anti-Fraud CentreLearn common scams and report fraud

Red flags: urgent threats of arrest or deportation, requests for gift cards or crypto, fake job offers that ask you to pay. Government agencies will not ask for payment by gift card.

Find work online

Free tools to build a resume, apply for jobs and prepare for interviews.

Job search & applying

  • Job Bank (Government of Canada)Official national job board and career tools

Tip: read each posting carefully, match your resume keywords to it, and keep a list of where you applied.

Free resume tools

  • Canva resume templatesFree, editable resume designs

Canadian resumes usually skip photo, age and marital status. Keep it to 1–2 pages and focus on results.

LinkedIn tips

  • LinkedIn Help CentreSet up and improve your profile

Add a clear photo, a headline with your target role, and your skills. Connect with people in your field and in Durham Region.

Virtual interview tips

  • Grow with GoogleFree career and digital skills training

Test your camera, mic and internet beforehand, find a quiet well-lit spot, and prepare short stories about your past work.
