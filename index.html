Community project proposal · Durham Region & Oshawa, ON

Free digital skills to help newcomers launch their life in Canada.

Four free, hands-on Saturday sessions covering computer basics, getting hired online, an intro to coding and AI tools, and digital rights. Loaner laptops for anyone who needs one.

Register your interestSee the sessions

Why this program

I came to Canada from Nigeria with a Mechanical Engineering degree from Obafemi Awolowo University, then retrained in tech by earning a Computer Programming diploma at Durham College. I know firsthand how much digital skills shape a newcomer's first steps here, and I want to share them freely with my community.

— Joseph Bepo, project organizer

The four sessions

Planned as four free Saturday sessions. Dates and venue to be announced.

1

Digital foundations

  • Computer basics
  • Setting up and using email
  • Online safety & spotting scams
2

Get hired online

  • Canadian-style resumes with free tools
  • Online job applications
  • LinkedIn basics
  • Virtual interviews
3

Intro to coding & AI tools

  • Build your first webpage
  • Using AI tools responsibly
4

Digital rights & next steps

  • Online privacy
  • Workplace rights basics
  • Links to Durham settlement services
  • One-on-one help

Who it's for

Newcomers living in Durham Region and Oshawa who want to build confidence online — no experience needed.

New to computers

Start from the basics at your own pace.

Job seekers

Learn the digital side of finding work in Canada.

Curious about tech

Try coding and AI tools for the first time.

Loaner laptops are available for participants without one.
